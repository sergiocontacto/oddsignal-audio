Independent audio software. Built in Barcelona.

ODDSIGNAL AUDIO is an independent audio software company based in Barcelona, Spain, created with a passion for sound, design and music production.

After more than 15 years of making music, we started ODDSIGNAL AUDIO with a clear purpose to create professional tools for producers who care deeply about their sound.

We believe great audio software should be powerful without feeling complicated. Every detail matters, from the sound and character of a plugin to the way it feels when you use it in a session.

Our products are designed to combine professional sound quality, intuitive workflows and distinctive visual design, giving producers the freedom to focus on what matters most: creating music.

EvoraVerb is the first release from ODDSIGNAL AUDIO, the beginning of a collection of tools built to shape, transform and inspire sound.

We are an independent company, and that gives us the freedom to take our time, experiment and build products with a clear vision rather than following trends.

This is just the beginning.
