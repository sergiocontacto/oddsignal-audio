Who We Are

ODDSIGNAL AUDIO is an independent audio tools company born in Barcelona, driven by a passion for professional-grade sound design and music production.

Founded by a music producer with 15+ years of experience in composition, production, mixing, and mastering, ODDSIGNAL AUDIO emerged from real-world production needs. We create tools that we ourselves would want to use – no compromises, no shortcuts.

Our philosophy is simple: great tools should inspire creativity, not distract from it. Every plugin we build combines advanced DSP technology with obsessive attention to detail in user interface design. We believe that premium audio tools should be accessible to independent producers and professionals alike.

EvoraVerb is our first creation – a testament to our commitment to quality. It's the beginning of a journey to develop a complete ecosystem of audio tools that producers trust for their most important work.

Technical Foundation

ODDSIGNAL AUDIO tools are built using modern DSP techniques and professional audio frameworks. We maintain strict standards for audio quality, CPU efficiency, and user experience across all platforms.

Our development process emphasizes real-world testing with professional producers, extensive A/B listening comparisons, and continuous optimization for both Windows and macOS platforms.
