Refund Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Not sure it will run in your setup? Ask us before you buy and we will tell you what we know. And if you buy anyway and it turns out not to work for you, write to us within 14 days and we will refund it.

The 14 day window

You have 14 days from the date of purchase to ask for a refund. We do not interrogate you about the reason. Tell us it did not fit your workflow and that is enough.

We will always try to fix the problem first, because usually there is one and it is fixable: a plugin the DAW is not scanning, a licence key that will not activate, a format we did not build for. Give us a chance to sort it out. If we cannot, you get your money back.

Your legal right of withdrawal

If you are a consumer in the European Union, the law gives you 14 days to withdraw from a distance purchase. For digital content delivered immediately there is an exception: that right ends once the download starts, provided you gave express consent to start it and acknowledged that you were giving up the right of withdrawal. Our checkout asks for exactly that consent before the download link is issued.

The 14 day policy described above is what we offer on top of that, of our own accord. Your statutory consumer rights over faulty or misdescribed goods are unaffected by anything on this page, and remain whatever the law of your country says they are.

When we may say no

  • More than 14 days have passed since the purchase.
  • The licence key has been shared, published or resold.
  • There is a pattern of buying and refunding the same product repeatedly.
  • You bought from another store. Those purchases are handled by the store that sold them, under their policy.

Being outside the window is not an automatic no. If something is genuinely broken, write to us anyway and we will look at it.

Bought somewhere other than this site

If you bought through KVR Audio or any other marketplace, the sale is theirs and so is the refund. Ask them first. If they send you back to us, forward us the correspondence and we will help.

How to ask

Email us with your order number and the email address you bought with. If there is a technical problem, tell us your operating system, your DAW and its version, and what the plugin does or does not do. That usually saves a whole exchange of messages.

How long it takes

We answer refund requests within two working days. Purchases from this site are processed by Lemon Squeezy as seller of record, so the refund is issued by them, back to the payment method you used. It usually lands in 5 to 10 working days depending on your bank, and the licence key stops working when it is issued.

Before you open a chargeback

Please write to us first. A chargeback takes weeks, costs us a fee on top of the refund, and gets you your money more slowly than simply asking. We have never refused a reasonable request.

Duplicate or failed charges

If you were charged twice, or charged for something that never arrived, tell us and we refund it in full straight away. The 14 day window does not apply to that.
