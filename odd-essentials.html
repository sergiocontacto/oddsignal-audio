Distortion

ODD DRIVE

Warmth, grit or total burn.

A saturation that stays musical from the first percent to the last, with the output level compensated so you judge the tone and not the volume.

  • 4x oversampled saturation
  • Level compensation
  • Mix control for parallel distortion

Reverb

ODD ROOM

Space for anything that sounds dry.

From a tight vocal booth to a wide open hall. Two knobs shape the room, and tone decides how bright the tail comes back.

  • Tails from 0.4 to 5 seconds
  • Pre-delay and width follow the room size
  • Tone control for dark or bright rooms

Presets

  • Default
  • Vocal Booth
  • Drum Room
  • Warm Hall
  • Bright Plate
  • Big Space

Bus compressor

ODD GLUE

One knob to hold it together.

Glue moves threshold and ratio at once, so a drum group or a whole mix settles into place without menus or guesswork.

  • Low end ignored by the detector below 90 Hz
  • Stereo-linked with automatic release
  • Mix control for parallel compression

Presets

  • Default
  • Mix Bus
  • Drum Bus
  • Vocal Hold
  • Parallel Smash
  • Bass Control

Tempo delay

ODD ECHO

Repeats that follow your song.

A tape-style delay locked to your project tempo. Each repeat gets darker and softer, and changing the time bends the sound instead of clicking.

  • Nine note values from 1/16 to 1/2
  • Filtered and saturated tape-style repeats
  • Smooth time changes

Presets

  • Quarter Tape
  • Vocal Throw
  • Triplet Run
  • Dub Cave
